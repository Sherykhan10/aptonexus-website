# Content guide

Default language: **US English**.

Write in a clear, confident, concise, business-oriented, technically capable, human and specific tone. Lead with business outcomes before technical jargon. Explain concrete capabilities without promising unverified results.

Avoid these phrases:
- Unlock the power of AI
- Revolutionize your business
- Cutting-edge solutions
- Transform your digital journey
- Unleash AI

Never invent facts, customers, testimonials, results, awards or certifications. Publish metrics only with verified evidence and permission where applicable. Request missing information instead of filling gaps with invented claims.

Business identity/contact/SEO belong in `content/site.ts`, services in `content/services.ts`, and projects in `content/projects.ts`. Keep future business copy in structured content, not duplicated across components. Keep documented facts synchronized when changing these sources.
