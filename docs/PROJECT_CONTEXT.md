# Project context

AptoNexus is an AI automation and custom software company / agency serving the United States and United Kingdom primarily, and other English-speaking countries secondarily.

## Services
- **AI Agents & Chatbots:** custom 24/7 agents trained on business knowledge for inquiries, lead qualification, customer support, text/voice interactions and related actions.
- **Workflow Automation:** end-to-end business automations connecting tools and reducing repetitive manual work and operational bottlenecks.
- **AI-Powered Web & Mobile Apps:** full-stack applications with AI functionality designed into the product.
- **Custom Integrations:** AI capabilities, APIs, webhooks and automation pipelines integrated into CRMs, ERPs, inboxes, proprietary applications and existing business systems.

## Contact and conversion
Business email: contact@aptonexus.com. WhatsApp details are authoritative in `site.contact.whatsapp`; update them only there. Both channels are enabled in `content/site.ts`. The initial conversion strategy is direct WhatsApp/email inquiries. Calendar booking is disabled, with provider and URL set to null; support for a future provider is reserved in configuration. The shared contact launcher, mobile navigation, footer, and contact page use this configuration. The inquiry tool prepares an email draft without a backend submission.

## Website goals
- Generate qualified business inquiries.
- Demonstrate technical competence and show real AI automation projects.
- Communicate services clearly and make WhatsApp/email contact easy.
- Later support booking/calendar scheduling.
- Establish a premium professional presence for US/UK clients.
